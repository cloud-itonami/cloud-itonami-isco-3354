# Business Model: Independent Government Licensing Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-3354`
- ISCO-08: `3354`
- Occupation: Government Licensing Officials
- Social impact: regulatory-transparency, administrative-efficiency, local-jobs

## Customer

- government licensing offices and agencies
- business/professional licensing bureaus

## Offer

- license-application intake and case-record filing
- licensing-review/inspection appointment scheduling
- licensing-review flagging for human officials (new applications, renewals,
  compliance-issue triggers)
- office-supply coordination

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-case coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no proposal may issue, deny, renew or revoke a license or permit — those
  decisions stay with a human licensing official, permanently, and no such
  op exists anywhere in this actor's allowlist
- every license-application case-file basis is independently verified
  against the registered office and case file, not asserted by the advisor
- a supply order above the office's registered cost ceiling always requires
  human sign-off
- an application flagged for licensing review is always surfaced to a
  human, never auto-resolved
- intake, scheduling and coordination records are auditable, not editable
